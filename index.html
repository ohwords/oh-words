Oh! Words

A new English word every day. Hear it, say it, and see how it's really used.

Hear it

See the pronunciation written out, listen to a real person say many of the words, and slow it down to catch every sound.

Say it

Pronounce the word and find out if you got it right. Your voice is checked privately on your device and never recorded.

Understand it

Clear, modern meanings, with example sentences that show how each word is used.

Make it a habit

Keep a daily streak, earn badges, set a reminder, and see the day's word in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Choose Everyday, Expanding or Rare words, or a mix of all three. Oh! Words has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it collects no personal information.
