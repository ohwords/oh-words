Say it says it can't hear me

Say it needs the microphone and speech recognition. Tap Open Settings under the word, or go to Settings > Apps > Oh! Words, and turn both on.

Listen uses a computer voice

Recordings of real people need an internet connection, and not every word has one. Otherwise the app uses your device's built-in voice.

I don't get my daily reminder

Turn on Daily reminder in the app's Settings tab and choose a time. If it still doesn't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Oh! Words in your device's Settings. Opening the app now and then keeps the reminders scheduled.

My widget shows a different word

Widgets follow the difficulty level chosen in the app's Settings tab. Opening the app refreshes them.

Where does my streak go if I delete the app?

Your streak and favourite words are saved only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.
