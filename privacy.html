Oh! Words

Privacy Policy · Last updated 4 November 2026

Oh! Words does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your favourite words, your daily streak, your chosen difficulty level and your reminder time are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you tap Say it to practise saying a word. Your voice is checked by Apple's speech recognition on your device. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Word recordings

Some words have a recording of a real person, which the app plays from Wikimedia Commons, the media library of Wikipedia and Wiktionary. To play it, your device asks Wikimedia's servers for the sound file, as any web browser would; this shares your device's IP address with Wikimedia but nothing about you or how you use the app. Their practices are described in the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. Without an internet connection, the app uses your device's built-in voice instead.

Reminders

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service or server is involved.

App Store statistics

Apple may share basic, anonymous statistics with us, such as how many people installed the app or how often it crashed, but only if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings. This information cannot identify you.

Children

Because Oh! Words collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
